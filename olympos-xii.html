Olympos XII

12 Deity Digimon protecting the Server Iliad realm

1. Jupitermon

Leader of the Olympos XII commanding lightning and justice.

2. Junomon

Partner of Jupitermon, guardian and threat detector of Iliad.

3. Neptunmon

Ruler of all aquatic Digimon and oceans in Server Iliad.

4. Ceresmon

Colossal floating island deity bringing fertility to the land.

5. Bacchusmon

God of feasts capable of brewing elixirs and neutralizing toxins.

6. Apollomon

Sun God harnessing high-energy thermal flame power.

7. Dianamon

Moon Goddess controlling ice and moving silently in shadows.

8. Vulcanusmon

Master blacksmith who forged legendary weapons for deities.

9. Marsmon

God of War mastering all forms of bare-handed martial arts.

10. Minervamon

Deity of Wisdom and Strategy wielding a giant broadsword.

11. Mercurymon

Fastest god in Iliad commanding spatial movement.

12. Venusmon

Goddess of Beauty and Love capable of pacifying malicious intent.
