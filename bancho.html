Bancho Group

Five Digimon holding the honorable "Bancho" title with unwavering pride

1. Bancholeomon

Lion clad in a Gakuran coat standing firm on justice. His sword Dankei cuts down wickedness.

2. Bancholillymon

Dressed in Sukeban delinquent fashion. Fiercely protects weak Digimon with unmatched tenacity.

3. Banchostingmon

Insectoid wearing a leather jacket, prioritizing honor in one-on-one duels.

4. Banchogolemon

Silent rock giant serving as an unbreakable shield for his allies.

5. Banchomamemon

Small street-gang style Digimon dominating close-range brawls.
