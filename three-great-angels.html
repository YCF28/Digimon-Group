Three Great Angels

The celestial triumvirate guarding the sacred Kernel of the Digital World

1. Seraphimon

Attribute: Vaccine | Type: Seraph / Celestial

The highest-ranking angel who strictly executes divine law. Clad in shining silver armor with ten golden wings, he executes judgment with Seven Heavens.

2. Cherubimon

Attribute: Vaccine / Virus | Type: Cherub / Celestial

Guardian of sacred knowledge, traditions, and lore. Possesses a beast-like holy form with horns, though highly susceptible to darkness if balance is lost.

3. Ophanimon

Attribute: Vaccine | Type: Throne / Celestial

The embodiment of divine love and mercy. Purifies fallen Digimon souls and spreads peace wielding the Eden's Javelin.
