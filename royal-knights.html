Royal Knights

The 13 Holy Knights guarding the sacred throne of the Digital World

1. Omnimon (Omegamon)

Attribute: Vaccine | Type: Holy Knight

Born from the fuse (Jogress) of WarGreymon and MetalGarurumon. Armed with the Grey Sword on his left arm and the Garuru Cannon on his right arm, he serves as a field commander of the Order.

2. Alphamon

Attribute: Vaccine | Type: Holy Knight

The secret leader who holds the "Empty Seat." Alphamon possesses the Alpha Inforce ability, allowing him to replay past moments of battle instantaneously.

3. Gallantmon (Dukemon)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Holy Knight

Despite being a Virus-attribute, he is a staunch defender of justice. Wields the holy lance Gram and the holy shield Aegis.

4. Magnamon

Attribute: Free | Type: Holy Knight

The only member who evolved through the power of Armor Digivolution (Digimental of Miracles). His golden armor grants virtually unmatched defensive durability.

5. UlforceVeedramon

Attribute: Vaccine | Type: Holy Knight

The swiftest among the Royal Knights. He masters the legendary Ulforce secret code, enabling near-instantaneous health regeneration in battle.

6. Jesmon

Attribute: Data | Type: Holy Knight

The 13th and latest knight to join. Accompanied by three sword spirits (At, René, and Pol), he possesses the power to rewrite the environmental laws of battle.

7. Gankoomon

Attribute: Data | Type: Holy Knight

Mentored Jesmon throughout his journey. Travels across the Digital World resolving chaos alongside his dragon aura Hinukamuy.

8. Examon

Attribute: Data | Type: Holy Knight / Dragon

Known as the "Dragon Emperor". Possesses a colossal body size requiring massive data capacity just to materialize.

9. Dynasmon

Attribute: Data | Type: Holy Knight

A dragon-themed knight with unyielding loyalty to his own convictions. Unleashes massive dragon energy attacks.

10. LordKnightmon (RhodoKnightmon)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Holy Knight

Monarch of all Knightmon. Governs under ruthless aesthetics, firmly believing that "Might is Right".

11. Craniamon

Attribute: Vaccine | Type: Holy Knight

The most chivalrous member boasting the highest physical defense. Armor modified directly by Yggdrasil, wielding the holy shield Avalon.

12. Sleipmon (Kentaurosmon)

Attribute: Vaccine | Type: Holy Knight

A six-legged centaur-like knight armored in Red Digizoid. Guards ancient relics hidden beneath frozen glaciers.

13. Leopardmon (Duftmon)

Attribute: Data | Type: Holy Knight

Supreme strategist of the Royal Knights. Can shift into Leopard Mode to transition from tactical command to close-quarters combat.

Close Allies: Sistermon Trio

While not official Royal Knights, the Sistermon sisters were saved by Gankoomon and served as trainers and escorts for Huckmon (Jesmon).

Sistermon Blanc (Child / Rookie)

Attribute: Vaccine | Type: Puppet

The shy and gentle youngest sister wearing a white bunny hood. Rescued alongside Noir by Gankoomon, she cared for Huckmon with kindness while attempting to restrain Noir's aggressive training methods.

Sistermon Noir (Adult / Champion)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Puppet

The energetic, hot-tempered eldest sister wearing a black cat hood. Served as Huckmon's sparring partner using her dual pistols Anthony to conduct harsh combat training.

Sistermon Ciel (Adult / Champion)

Attribute: Data | Type: Puppet

The calm twin sister of Noir wearing a blue mouse hood. Unlike Blanc and Noir who traveled alongside Huckmon, Ciel wanders the Digital World independently, investigating anomalies and reporting intel back to Gankoomon and Jesmon.
