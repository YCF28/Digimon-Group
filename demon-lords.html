Seven Deadly Demon Lords

Seven demon sovereigns ruling the Dark Area, embodying mortal sins

1. Lucemon Chaos Mode (Sin: Pride)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Demon Lord

Fallen form of Lucemon wielding both holy light and utter darkness. Functions as the unofficial leader of the Demon Lords.

2. Beelzemon (Sin: Gluttony)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Demon Lord

A lone-wolf demon rider piloting the giant motorcycle Behemoth while wielding dual shotguns named Berenjena.

3. Daemon (Sin: Wrath)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Demon Lord

A former high-ranking angel cast into darkness after leading a rebellion. Driven by eternal, burning rage.

4. Leviamon (Sin: Envy)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Demon Lord

A gigantic crocodile monster sleeping in the depths of the Net Ocean. His colossal jaws are said to be capable of swallowing the entire Digital World.

5. Lilithmon (Sin: Lust)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Demon Lord

Known as the "Goddess of Darkness". Uses mesmerising beauty and lethal rot from her Nazar Nail to corrupt foes.

6. Belphemon Rage Mode (Sin: Sloth)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Demon Lord

Sleeps for thousands of years (Sleep Mode). Upon awakening into Rage Mode, he transforms into an uncontainable, destructive beast.

7. Barbamon (Sin: Greed)

Attribute: Virus | Type: Demon Lord

A cunning wizard-like demon obsessed with hoarding all data, treasure, and artifacts across the Digital World.
