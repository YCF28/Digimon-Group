Four Holy Beasts & Fanglongmon

Four Sacred Beasts protecting cardinal realms under Fanglongmon

Central Leader: Fanglongmon

Attribute: Data | Type: Yellow Dragon

An eight-headed golden dragon residing at the center of the Digital World. Coated in indestructible Huanglong Ore scales.

1. Azulongmon (Qinglongmon) - Guardian of the East

Attribute: Data | Type: Holy Dragon

Blue dragon harnessing lightning and thunder. Member of both Holy Beasts and the Four Great Dragons.

2. Zhuqiaomon - Guardian of the South

Attribute: Virus | Type: Holy Bird

A giant phoenix engulfed in intense flames. The most aggressive guardian, holding deep disdain toward humans.

3. Baihumon - Guardian of the West

Attribute: Data | Type: Holy Beast

A white tiger with steel eyes commanding metal powers. Though the youngest, he possesses the highest physical strength.

4. Ebonwumon (Xuanwumon) - Guardian of the North

Attribute: Vaccine | Type: Holy Beast

A dual-headed turtle bearing a giant tree, commanding water and ice. The oldest guardian with a calm demeanor.
